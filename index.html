In English

An unofficial side panel for Gemini in Chrome and Edge: summarise the page, work through the questions in a PDF book step by step, keep a study plan and a notebook of wrong answers. No account and no server: your data stays in your browser. Syncing, if you turn it on, uses a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Read the privacy policy.
